## About Dave Cornock

I'm Dave Graham, a UEFA B Licensed football coach with a broad coaching background that spans all levels of the game. My journey has taken me from grassroots football and local leagues, through the Junior Premier League (JPL) and Hellenic League, to coaching elite Tier 2 women's university teams and UDA overseas students.

### My Philosophy

> "Your journey is unique. Your development should be too."

I'm passionate about helping players and coaches unlock their full potential. Whether it's delivering tailored 1-to-1 sessions, designing structured training plans, or mentoring coaches throughout a season, I'm here to support your growth and development.

### Coaching Philosophy

**Individual Focus**: Every player is unique with their own strengths, challenges, and goals. Our approach is tailored to bring out the best in each individual.

**Technical Excellence**: Building solid technical foundations while developing tactical understanding that will serve players throughout their football journey.

**Holistic Development**: Football is a vehicle for personal growth. We focus on confidence, discipline, and life skills that extend beyond the pitch.

### Credentials & Qualifications

- UEFA B License qualified
- FA Level 2 Coaching Badge
- Semi-professional playing experience
- Grassroots to elite level coaching
- DBS checked and safeguarding certified
- First Aid qualified
